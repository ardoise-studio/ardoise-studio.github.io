Privacy policy for Loulaine

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Loulaine is published by Florian Giroud, individual entrepreneur (France). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your projects and patterns stay on your phone. The app only sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What stays on your phone

Your projects, counters, reminders and the PDF patterns you import are stored only on your device. We have no server and we never receive them. If your phone's backup is turned on (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your phone's system may include the app's data in that backup. This is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, and we cannot access it.

What we don't do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no device or advertising identifier.
  • No sale of data, to anyone.
  • The app connects to the internet only to talk to the App Store or Google Play when you buy or restore the unlock, and to send the anonymous statistics described below, if you leave them on.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the app is used and improve it, Loulaine sends a few anonymous events (for example "a project was created" or "the unlock screen was shown") to Aptabase, an analytics service that stores this data in the European Union (Germany). An event contains only its name, simple categories (such as a PDF's number of pages, in ranges), the app and system version, the language, the version of the statistics library and whether it is a test build, a random session number that changes often, and the country, worked out from the internet address. The internet address itself is not stored. No device or advertising identifier, no name or email, and nothing from your projects (patterns, file names, notes, reminders) is ever sent, and the statistics are not linked to you. You can turn them off at any time in the app: Settings › Share anonymous usage statistics.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details or your Apple or Google account. The stores give us sales reports (for example, the country and the price of a sale) that do not identify you to us.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your phone's settings to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may send us anonymous crash reports. They help us fix bugs and contain no personal content from the app. You can turn this off in your phone's settings at any time.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you, and we delete the conversation once it is no longer needed.

Children

The app is not aimed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

We hold no data that identifies you: the anonymous statistics are not linked to you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To stop sending them, turn the setting off. To remove all the app's data, delete the app (and, if you want, its backup in your phone's settings). For any question, or to exercise your rights under the GDPR, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
