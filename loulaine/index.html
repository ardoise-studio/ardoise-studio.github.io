Loulaine Français

Keep your place in every pattern.

Loulaine puts your PDF pattern and your row counters on one screen. Tap + at the end of a row: the counter goes up and the highlight moves down to the next line of your pattern.

  • Pattern and counters togetherOpen your PDF patterns from Files, Mail or Safari. The highlight follows your rows, page after page.
  • Repeats and remindersExtra counters for repeats and decreases, and reminders on the rows that matter: "decrease on row 12".
  • No lost countA big + you can tap without looking, undo for every change, and a lock against accidental taps.
  • Knitting and crochet, in English and FrenchWritten for both crafts and both languages.

Free for one project, with every feature. One price, paid once, unlocks unlimited projects for life. No subscription, no ads, no account. Your projects and patterns stay on your phone.
