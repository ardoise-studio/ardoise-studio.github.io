Support

Answers to the most common questions. Something else? Write to us, we read every message.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Loulaine, go to Settings › Restore purchase (the button is also on the unlock screen). Use the same Apple ID, or the same Google account, as for the purchase. You paid once: restoring is always free.

How do I move my projects to a new phone?

Your purchase comes back with Restore purchase (see above).

Your projects and patterns are stored on your phone only, never on our servers. On iPhone, they move with your phone backup: when you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, Loulaine finds your projects again. If you set up the new phone as new, they stay on the old one.

How do I turn off the anonymous statistics?

Go to Settings › Share anonymous usage statistics and turn it off. Loulaine then stops sending them at once. These statistics never contain your patterns, file names or anything that identifies you: see the privacy policy.

How do I import a PDF pattern?

Tap New project, then Choose a PDF and pick it in Files (iCloud Drive and other storage apps appear there too). From Safari, Mail or Files, you can also tap Share on a PDF and choose Loulaine.

Loulaine keeps its own copy, so moving or deleting the original does not affect your project. Password-protected PDFs cannot be opened.
